**Primary Name: HIVE³** (pronounced "Hive Cubed")

- **9 × 9 × 9 = 729** nodes in the hypercube matrix

- **28 agents** (2+8 = 10, 1+0 = 1, but 28+9 = 37, 3+7 = 10... ultimate digital root 9)

## Why HIVE³ Won

1. **Concise**: Short, memorable, easy to type
2. **Mathematical**: ³ immediately communicates the 9×9×9 structure
3. **Visual**: The superscript 3 creates visual interest
4. **Extensible**: Can be styled as HIVE3, HIVE³, or HIVE CUBED
5. **Domain-Friendly**: hive3.io, hivecube.com available
6. **Logo-Friendly**: Cube + bee combination works perfectly
7. **Tagline Compatible**: "The 729 Detective Agency"
8. **Multi-lingual**: "Hive" and "3" are universal
