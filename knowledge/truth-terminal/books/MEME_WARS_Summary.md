# MEME WARS: The Creative Destruction of Neoclassical Economics - Kalle Lasn

## 📖 Book Information
- **Author:** Kalle Lasn (Estonian-Canadian activist, filmmaker, author)
- **Publisher:** Seven Stories Press
- **Publication Date:** 2012
- **ISBN:** 978-1-60980-473-2
- **Context:** Part of Adbusters Media Foundation's meme warfare campaign

## 🎯 Core Thesis
Neoclassical economics is a flawed paradigm that ignores externalities and perpetuates consumer capitalism. A "meme war" is needed to shift Western society toward more sustainable, authentic values.

## 📝 Key Concepts

### 1. Culture Jamming
- **Definition:** Acts designed to subvert mainstream commercial society through disruption
- **Origin:** Lasn's "first culture jam" - jamming a shopping cart mechanism with a bent coin
- **Method:** Taking conventional marketing techniques and applying them to anti-consumerism
- **Goal:** Interrupt the spectacle, promote awareness, shift meaning

### 2. The Spectacle (Debord's Concept)
- **Definition:** The totality of mediated images, environments, and experiences that constitute modern reality
- **Lasn's Application:** Thousands of images encountered daily that shape consciousness
- **Problem:** Spectacle colonizes authentic experience
- **Solution:** Detournement (rerouting spectacle to subvert meaning)

### 3. Detournement
- **Definition:** Rerouting spectacular images, environments, and events to reverse their meaning
- **Origin:** French Situationists (Guy Debord)
- **Technique:** Hijack commercial imagery, repurpose for counter-messages
- **Example:** Subverting advertisements to critique consumerism

### 4. Meme Warfare
- **Definition:** Strategic deployment of ideas as weapons in cultural conflict
- **Goal:** Shift consciousness away from consumer capitalism
- **Tactics:**
  - Viral campaigns
  - Provocative imagery
  - Disruptive stunts
  - Counter-advertising
- **Target:** The economic paradigm itself

### 5. Creative Destruction
- **Definition:** The process by which new innovations destroy old paradigms
- **Lasn's Use:** Applying Schumpeter's concept to economic philosophy
- **Application:** Replace neoclassical economics with ecological/alternative economics

## 💡 Major Themes

### Economics as Ideology
- **Critique:** Neoclassical economics ignores externalities (pollution, inequality, unsustainability)
- **Problem:** Economics education presents flawed assumptions as objective truth
- **Solution:** Expose the contested, political nature of economic theories
- **Call to Action:** Students should become "agitators, provocateurs, meme warriors"

### Consumer Capitalism as Evil
- **Core Thesis:** Consumerism is the fundamental evil of modern era
- **Mechanism:** Marketing co-opts authenticity and freedom of expression
- **Result:** People value possessions over experiences
- **Resistance:** Culture jamming, meme warfare, detournement

### Authenticity and Individuality
- **Problem:** Brand-based identity replaces authentic selfhood
- **Solution:** Reclaim individual expression from commercial appropriation
- **Method:** Demarketing, demarking, disrupting consumption rituals

### The Adbusters Model
- **Structure:** Small core team (10 employees), large global following
- **Impact:** 40,000 paper subscribers + 30,000 online subscribers
- **Strategy:** Slick graphics, vibrant language, provocative stunts
- **Claim:** "Hub of global activism – communication model of the future"

## 🎓 Kalle Lasn Biography

### Personal Background
- **Born:** March 24, 1942, Tallinn, Estonia
- **Early Life:** Refugee camp after WWII, resettled in Australia
- **Education:** Degree in applied mathematics, Australia
- **Career:** Market research in Tokyo (1960s), moved to Vancouver (1970)

### Professional Work
- **Adbusters:** Co-founded magazine (1980s)
- **Film:** 20 years of documentary filmmaking for PBS and NFB
- **Books:** Culture Jam, Design Anarchy, Meme Wars
- **Activism:** Occupy Wall Street origin, global culture jamming campaigns

### Notable Campaigns
- **Buy Nothing Day:** Annual protest against consumerism
- **Black Friday Protests:** Disrupt shopping on Black Friday
- **Occupy Wall Street:** Original idea, though Lasn doesn't claim ownership
- **Counter-Advertisements:** Anti-consumerism messaging
